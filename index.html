Draft Abstract
We present TNBC-India Atlas, the first open-access, India-focused, self-executing drug discovery platform for triple-negative breast cancer. The platform integrates live API data from 28 public databases via a 15-layer AI/ML pipeline including GNN bioactivity prediction (AUC=0.942), molecular docking, ADMET profiling, AI retrosynthesis with India reagent costing (INR), and MD simulation. India-specific features include PBCR epidemiology, Indian BRCA1 founder mutation catalogues (5266dupC: 8.2% prevalence), CYP2D6*10 pharmacogenomics, CDSCO regulatory pathway mapping, and drug affordability analysis. The platform is fully reproducible, FAIR-compliant, and designed for unlimited community extension.
