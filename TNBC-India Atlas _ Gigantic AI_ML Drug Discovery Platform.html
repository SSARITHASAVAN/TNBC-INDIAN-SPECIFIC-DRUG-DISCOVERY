TNBC-India Atlas: A Gigantic AI/ML Drug Discovery Platform for Triple-Negative Breast Cancer with Indian-Specific Genomic, Clinical, and Epidemiological Integration

          
S. Sarithasavan et al. · TNBC-India Atlas Consortium · 2024

          
We present TNBC-India Atlas, the first open-access, India-focused, end-to-end drug discovery platform for triple-negative breast cancer. Integrating 28 data sources across genomics, transcriptomics, drug response, and chemical biology, the platform employs a 15-layer AI/ML pipeline including GNN-based bioactivity prediction (AUC=0.942), multi-target molecular docking, ADMET profiling, retrosynthetic analysis, and molecular dynamics simulation. Indian-specific features include ICMR/PBCR epidemiology, Indian BRCA1/2 founder mutation catalogues, CDSCO regulatory pathway mapping, and drug affordability scoring. The platform is fully reproducible via containerized Nextflow workflows, FAIR-compliant, and designed for unlimited community extension.
